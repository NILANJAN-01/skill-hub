# Code of conduct

We want skill-hub to be a friendly place to contribute, whatever your experience or background.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind; accept feedback the same way.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, use the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** (private reporting), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This is a short statement in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
